# Alert Messages

These messages are demonstration text for the Arogya prototype. They are not medical diagnoses or treatment recommendations.

## Green

- “All selected indicators are within the demonstration range.”
- “Continue routine monitoring and follow the mission plan.”
- “No active warning indicators are recorded.”
- “Wellbeing indicators appear stable for this check.”

## Yellow

- “One indicator is near or outside the demonstration threshold.”
- “Rest if appropriate, hydrate if appropriate, and repeat the measurement later.”
- “Consider reducing nonessential exertion and reviewing your recent sleep, nutrition, and activity.”
- “Use a relaxation or mindfulness activity if helpful, and check again later.”

## Orange

- “Multiple indicators or persistent changes are outside the demonstration range.”
- “Reduce avoidable workload, rest, and contact the crew medical lead.”
- “Do not increase exercise intensity while symptoms or abnormal readings persist.”
- “Record the time, readings, symptoms, and actions taken for the crew medical lead.”

## Red

- “This pattern requires urgent review under the selected demonstration rules.”
- “Stop nonessential strenuous activity, rest, and contact the crew medical lead.”
- “Contact mission control if needed, following established communication procedures.”
- “Do not rely on Arogya for diagnosis or treatment; use mission medical procedures and professional support.”

## Symptom-specific messages

- Headache: “A headache is recorded. Rest, hydrate if appropriate, and repeat the check later; contact the crew medical lead if it persists or worsens.”
- Dizziness: “Dizziness is recorded. Sit or lie down safely, avoid hazardous tasks, and contact the crew medical lead if it persists or worsens.”
- Nausea: “Nausea is recorded. Rest, follow the mission nutrition plan if tolerated, and contact the crew medical lead if it persists or worsens.”
- Pain: “Pain is recorded. Avoid increasing activity that worsens it and contact the crew medical lead if it persists, worsens, or limits safe movement.”
- Shortness of breath: “Shortness of breath is recorded. Stop strenuous activity, rest, and contact the crew medical lead or mission control as needed.”
- Vision changes: “Vision changes are recorded. Avoid safety-critical visual tasks where possible and contact the crew medical lead if changes persist or worsen.”
- Anxiety: “Anxiety is recorded. Use a relaxation or mindfulness activity, talk with a crew member, and contact the crew medical lead if it persists or affects safety.”
- Low mood: “Low mood is recorded. Rest, connect with a crew member, use a relaxation activity, and contact the crew medical lead if it persists or affects safety.”
