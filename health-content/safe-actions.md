# Safe Actions

These are general, non-clinical actions for the Arogya prototype. They do not replace professional medical care, flight surgeons, or mission-control medical support.

## General actions

- Drink water if appropriate for the mission plan and personal circumstances.
- Rest and reduce nonessential physical or cognitive workload.
- Repeat a measurement later to see whether a reading or symptom changes.
- Adjust exercise intensity rather than continuing an activity that causes unusual or worsening symptoms.
- Follow the mission nutrition plan.
- Use a relaxation or mindfulness activity.
- Talk with a crew member for support or assistance.
- Contact the crew medical lead when symptoms persist, worsen, or affect safe task performance.
- Contact mission control if needed, following established communication procedures.

## When to escalate

Seek crew medical review or mission-control support when there is:

- Severe or worsening pain, shortness of breath, dizziness, nausea, or vision changes.
- A major or sudden change in wellbeing, mobility, mood, or ability to perform essential tasks.
- Repeated abnormal readings or multiple warning indicators.
- A safety concern, including inability to perform required duties safely.
- Any situation where the crew medical lead or mission-control procedures direct escalation.

## What Arogya does not do

Arogya does not provide medication advice, diagnoses, treatment instructions, or clinical thresholds for real-world operational use. It is a demonstration decision-support tool.
