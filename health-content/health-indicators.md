# Health Indicators

These definitions and units are for the Arogya decision-support prototype. Thresholds and alerts are demonstration values and require validation by qualified medical professionals before real-world use.

## Measurements

| Indicator | Definition | Unit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resting heart rate | Heart beats per minute while awake and at rest | bpm | Record after several minutes of quiet rest, using the same method where possible. |
| Blood pressure | Pressure of blood against artery walls, recorded as systolic over diastolic pressure | mmHg | Example: 120/80 mmHg. |
| Body temperature | Core or approximate body temperature | Celsius | Use the same measurement site and device where possible. |
| Blood sugar | Concentration of glucose in blood | mg/dL | Record the value and time relative to food, activity, or sleep if known. |
| Sleep duration | Total time asleep during a sleep period | hours | Record estimated or tracked sleep duration. |
| Mood | Self-reported emotional state | 1–5 | 1 = very low, 2 = low, 3 = neutral, 4 = good, 5 = very good. |
| Fatigue | Self-reported tiredness or lack of energy | 1–5 | 1 = no fatigue, 2 = mild, 3 = moderate, 4 = marked, 5 = severe. |
| Hydration | Self-reported fluid intake or hydration status | 1–5 | 1 = very low, 2 = low, 3 = adequate, 4 = good, 5 = very good. |
| Exercise minutes | Minutes of planned physical activity completed | minutes | Record aerobic, resistance, or combined activity as applicable. |

## Symptoms

Symptoms are recorded as present or absent, with an optional severity rating where the interface provides one.

| Symptom | What to record |
|---|---|
| Headache | Presence, location if known, severity, and duration. |
| Dizziness | Presence, triggers, severity, and whether standing or movement worsens it. |
| Nausea | Presence, severity, and association with food, motion, or activity. |
| Pain | Presence, body area, severity, and whether it limits activity. |
| Shortness of breath | Presence, severity, triggers, and effect on activity. |
| Vision changes | Presence, type, duration, and whether it affects reading, navigation, or tasks. |
| Anxiety | Presence, severity, triggers, and effect on sleep or work. |
| Low mood | Presence, severity, duration, and effect on daily activities. |

## Demonstration alert levels

| Level | General meaning | Example response |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Indicators are within the selected demonstration range | Continue routine monitoring and mission activities. |
| Yellow | One indicator is near or slightly outside the selected demonstration range | Rest if appropriate, hydrate if appropriate, and repeat the measurement later. |
| Orange | Multiple indicators or persistent changes are outside the selected demonstration range | Reduce avoidable exertion, rest, and contact the crew medical lead. |
| Red | Severe symptoms, safety concerns, or urgent patterns are present | Stop nonessential strenuous activity, rest, and contact the crew medical lead or mission control as needed. |

Arogya does not diagnose conditions, recommend medication, or provide treatment instructions.
