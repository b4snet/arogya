Arogya is a decision-support prototype created for the NASA Space Apps Challenge.

It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or clinical advice. It does not replace professional medical care, flight surgeons, or mission-control medical support.

Health thresholds shown in this prototype are demonstration thresholds and must be validated by qualified medical professionals before real-world use.

Health entries are stored locally on the user’s device for demonstration purposes. No health data is transmitted to a server in this prototype.
